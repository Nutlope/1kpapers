# Research Atlas

The atlas organizes one year of AI papers into a small browsable hierarchy.

## Language

**Topic area**:
One of the eight broad parent categories used to navigate the atlas.
_Avoid_: Editorial path, shelf

**Collection**:
A focused research subject nested under exactly one topic area.
_Avoid_: Sub-topic, child topic

**Primary topic**:
The single collection used as a paper's main editorial assignment.
_Avoid_: Category
